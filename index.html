About BlackVault

Why BlackVault exists

Enduring enterprises begin with a clear view of something others have not yet acted on. BlackVault exists to turn that view into disciplined, durable business.

A group in formation

BlackVault is built on a long-term plan rather than a short-term idea. We enter a sector only after we understand it, commit capital and effort only where we can add real judgement, and hold ourselves to the standards of governance and execution expected of an institution.

Our method

Our outlook is entrepreneurial and our method is disciplined. For every opportunity we ask what must be true for it to endure, and we are prepared to decline when the answer is uncertain.

Philosophy and approach

How we think about capital

We enter a sector only after we understand it.

Each area of interest begins as research. We study the sector, test what must be true for a business to endure, and commit capital and effort only where we can add real judgement. Where the answer is uncertain, we decline.

Research-driven

Conviction is earned through work. We prefer primary evidence and first-hand understanding to consensus.

Independent judgement

We form our own view and are prepared to hold it, or abandon it, when the evidence changes.

Intelligent capital allocation

Capital is finite and every commitment has an opportunity cost. We allocate to the best use, not the most visible one.

Risk awareness

We start by asking what could go wrong, how badly, and whether we could withstand it.

Strategic positioning

We seek positions that strengthen over time through partnerships, relationships and operating insight.

Long-term value creation

We measure success across cycles and decades, and avoid decisions that borrow from the future.

Disciplined execution

A sound idea is only the beginning. Value is made in the details of delivery, governance and follow-through.
