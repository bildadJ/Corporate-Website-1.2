# BlackVault main website

Static site for **www.blackvault.co.ke** (stage-two refinement).

- `/` homepage: hero, Why BlackVault exists, Philosophy, Areas of strategic interest, BlackVault Intelligence (Sector Explorer, Capital and Pipeline, Research Centre, Publications and Briefing), Vision, Contact
- `/corporate/` identity, vision, mission, governance and principles, status
- `assets/css/site.css` all design tokens and components (palette at the top)
- `assets/js/site.js` mobile menu, intelligence tabs (hash-addressable: #sector-explorer, #capital-pipeline, #research-centre, #publications)

Vision (exact, final): To build enterprises of lasting economic value.
It appears in the hero, Vision section, Corporate page, footer, page descriptions, Open Graph metadata and the Organization JSON-LD.

Links to finance.blackvault.co.ke and agri.blackvault.co.ke work once those sites are live. See DEPLOY.md.
